## 🎮 Game Overview

In **Monkey Mart**, your mission is to build, manage, and expand your very own supermarket empire. Starting from humble beginnings with just a single stall selling fresh bananas, you will gradually unlock new products, expand your store layout, and automate your workflow to become the ultimate grocery tycoon.

With its vibrant, cartoonish visuals, smooth animations, and intuitive controls, the game delivers a highly satisfying loop of harvesting, stocking, and earning.

---

## 🚀 Core Gameplay & Features

### 1. The Supermarket Loop
* **Harvesting Goods:** Run over to the fields to harvest fresh bananas, plant corn, and gather dairy items like milk and eggs as your mart grows.
* **Stocking Shelves:** Carry your harvested items to the display shelves to keep them fully stocked for hungry animal shoppers.
* **Cashier Management:** Head over to the checkout counter to collect cash from customers once they finish shopping.

### 2. Steady Progression & Upgrades
Every coin you collect can be reinvested into your business. As you progress, you can unlock:
* **New Products:** Expand your inventory from basic fruits to advanced products, including popcorn, baked treats, and more.
* **Store Expansions:** Open up entirely new sections of the market to accommodate a larger variety of goods.
* **Character Upgrades:** Spend your earnings to increase your monkey's running speed and carrying capacity to operate more efficiently.

### 3. Hire and Automate (Idle Mechanics)
As your supermarket grows, it becomes too big for just one monkey to handle! You can hire:
* **Assistant Monkeys:** To automatically harvest crops and restock shelves.
* **Cashiers:** To manage the registers and collect money, allowing you to focus on high-level management and expanding the store.

## 🕹️ Controls

Monkey Mart features incredibly simple and intuitive controls, making it accessible on both desktop and mobile devices:
* **Keyboard:** Use the **Arrow keys** or **W, A, S, D** to move your monkey character.
* **Mouse:** Click and drag the mouse in the direction you want to move.
* **Mobile/Touch:** Drag your finger across the screen to control the character.
